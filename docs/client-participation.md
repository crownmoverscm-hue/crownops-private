# Client thank-you options

This feature is a manual choice near the end of a service, before issuing or collecting the final invoice. It never sends a campaign automatically. Review links can also be shared manually after the service.

## Owner setup

1. Open **Operating rules → $50 optional video → Configure**. Supply the reviewed English and French publication permission, a version, permitted channels and approval note. Select whether the $50 reduces the price before taxes or credits the final bill after taxes. Activate only after this wording and treatment have been reviewed.
2. Open **Aftercare → Review links & draw management**. Check the two Google links and label each actual business profile clearly. Staff must choose the profile that handled the move; clients should not be asked to review both profiles for the same experience.
3. To run the giveaway, choose **Set up a six-month draw**. Supply the first entry date, a draw date after the calculated six-month period, and reviewed English and French contest rules. Rules need eligibility, one entry per person, a genuine free-entry method, one CA$500 prize, dates, selection/odds, claim and applicable skill-testing requirements, and privacy/contact information. Have the rules reviewed for the actual territory and promotion before opening it. The app does not certify legal compliance.
4. Publish the generated **public free-entry link** wherever the giveaway is advertised. It works without being a customer, posting a review or supplying a video. The private client link is not the only entry method.

Existing live data is preserved. The new draw starts closed until an owner opens a period with rules. This release does not invent terms or mark them legally reviewed.

## Mover or sales walkthrough

1. Open an **In progress** or **Completed** service → **Video, review & free draw**, or open its draft invoice → **Client thank-you options**.
2. Choose the Google profile that handled the service.
3. Ask neutrally: “If you’d like to share an honest 30–60 second video about your move, there’s an optional $50 video credit. A Google review is entirely separate and earns no reward. If our free draw is open, you can enter without doing either.” Do not screen for positive reviewers, pressure someone to review on the spot or request a specific star rating.
4. Choose **Create client link**, then **Share link** or **Copy client link**. The link opens once and expires in 12 hours; after opening, the client can refresh that browser. Opening it on your own device uses it, so create a new link before sharing. Prefer the client’s own phone.
5. The client records or uploads a 30–180 second MP4/MOV/WebM video, up to 100 MB. It is processed privately. Then the client chooses publication channels, reads the terms and signs their own permission. A positive opinion, social post and Google review are not conditions.
6. Finish the service and prepare its draft invoice. Choose **Refresh client progress → Apply $50 credit**. Check the updated invoice before issuing it or collecting payment. The credit can be applied only once per move project and cannot be applied after the final invoice is issued or a final payment is recorded. Deposits remain separate.
7. Review sharing is independent: **Copy review link**, or **Prepare review email → review the exact recipient/message → Send this email now**. A private video link also has an email preview. Nothing is sent by opening the panel or preparing a draft. Uncertain delivery is held for review rather than resent automatically.

## Client choices

- Optional honest video with explicit publication permission: $50 credit under the approved terms.
- Optional honest Google review: no compensation and no required rating.
- When a draw is open, independent free entry for the $500 prize: no purchase, review, video or social post required.

The public and private pages support English and French. No testimonial is published automatically. Compensation must be disclosed whenever an incentivized testimonial is used. Existing portal permission-withdrawal handling remains available; clients can also contact the office.

## Crew review counts

A manager or owner can record an individual published Google review, its actual rating and evidence linking it to a completed service. The assigned crew receives the same verified service credit. **My Portal → My client feedback** displays the employee’s counts for January–June or July–December. All real ratings may be recorded. A link click is not counted as a review.

Google Business Profile review ingestion is not connected by this release. Matching is manual. These statistics do not automatically pay the previously discussed five-star employee prizes; no review quota or rating-based payout is enabled.

## Six-month draw administration

Each period is opened explicitly. Owners can pause or resume entries from Manage entries, with an audit reason; existing entries and published dates/rules are preserved. An owner reviews entries against the published rules, including duplicate-person and eligibility checks; email uniqueness alone does not prove a unique eligible person. Entry addresses are private to owners and are not added to marketing lists. Entry submission does not send an email or claim verification.

On or after the draw date, every entry must be marked Eligible or Ineligible with evidence. **Select one random winner** selects a provisional winner from the eligible roster and records the roster hash and candidate count. It cannot be rerun or silently edited. Verify the claim and any skill-testing requirement before paying the prize externally. **Record qualified winner’s payment** records that payment reference; it never transfers money. If a selected entrant cannot qualify or claim, the owner must resolve that situation under the reviewed rules; alternate-winner automation is not implemented.

## Delivery and limits

- No automatic pre-service or post-service review/video campaign. Legacy automatic review drafts are retired by the worker.
- Use the app’s connected email account to send. Native phone sharing can hand the link to the phone’s messaging app; CrownOps does not send SMS through this feature.
- Staff outside the assigned crew cannot use crew financial actions, even if they have planning privileges. Owner-only draw settings, entrant details and prize records are enforced server-side.
- All tests use fictional clients and mocked email delivery. No actual review, client message or prize payment is generated by the test suite.
