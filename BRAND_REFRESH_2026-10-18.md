# Crown Movers brand refresh — 0.12.0-rc1

The approved design uses a charcoal sidebar and client header, white work areas, warm neutral backgrounds, and solid #C4510A primary actions with white labels. The chosen button colours have a calculated contrast ratio of 4.64:1. The supplied Crown Movers crown pixels are embedded unchanged in the application icon; desktop, phone and install icons share that source.

Staff and client forms use 18px input text, larger readable labels, clear keyboard focus and generous action targets. Dense supporting text is enlarged while preserving responsive table cards and the existing quote, schedule, permissions and billing behavior. Client and staff email wrappers share the new colours. Email and accounting activation settings remain unchanged.

Before broad launch: approve the sender identity and do a real client-email walkthrough; connect and validate the intended production QuickBooks company/payment setup; enroll Owner/Manager MFA; review real truck safety, employee pay and company information; plan sufficient media storage and run a small team pilot. Do not mark real safety checks, payment receipts or client consent complete without evidence.
