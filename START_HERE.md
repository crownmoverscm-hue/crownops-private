# Start here: the CrownOps team process

This guide describes release 0.11.0-rc1. Confirm that version is deployed before using the new controls. See WORKFLOW_RELEASE_2026-10-18.md for the remaining launch checks.

## Sales

1. Open **Today** or **Sales & clients**. Start with callback requests, submitted client details and due follow-ups. Claim the inquiry so one person owns the next step.
2. Check the client and service before adding another record. Quote forms, calls and messages keep their source; ambiguous incoming messages need an explicit match in **Connections**.
3. Use **Request missing details**. Review and send the prepared email in **Communications**. The private moving form starts with the information already known.
4. Review the client's submitted information. Resolve commercial changes before preparing or sending a quote.
5. Prepare the estimate. Check the actual crew/truck requirements, local transportation fee or long-distance assumptions, materials, billing details and arrival window. The itemized view permits manual editing; calculator suggestions still need review.
6. Review and send. Use the recorded estimate opens, client response and next task to choose a helpful follow-up. An open count is context, not proof the client has read everything.
7. After acceptance, check date availability and payment arrangements. Coordinate confirmation and assignment with management. An accepted estimate remains tentative until booking requirements are met.

## Client

Open the private email link to check the moving file. Complete the missing information, review the prefilled details, upload photos/videos or paste a shared folder link, and click **Submit my details**. Check that the folder permits the team to view it. Request a callback from the same file when a conversation is easier.

Open the estimate from that file and review the details before accepting. A requested move date does not reserve the day by itself. After service, open the invoice from the email or moving file. A billing contact may receive an invoice-only link. If that link has expired, request a fresh one on the access page; it goes to the approved recipient.

## Manager / lead

1. Review **Today**, unclaimed work, client changes, payment exceptions and the schedule.
2. Mark a genuinely full date closed before another booking is made. Review existing assignments separately.
3. Confirm the booking only after the estimate, availability and payment arrangement are ready. Assign actual employees and truck; a Manager can also be assigned as a crew lead.
4. Check your own **My Jobs** as well as the company schedule. Review the crew brief and publish the company's real crew protocols in the shared guide.
5. Review requested extras and record the client's agreement before approving a price change. Resolve ambiguous deposits, payment discrepancies and uncertain email delivery using the management forms.
6. Keep expenses private in **Expenses** and review account/security settings with the Owner.

## Crew

1. Open **My Jobs**. Use **All Jobs** for permitted company visibility; work controls apply to jobs assigned to you.
2. Open the job and review the assigned team and truck, route/access information, materials, photos/folder links and **Crew brief & checklist**.
3. Complete the assigned truck's pre-use check. Record defects accurately and follow the company's reviewed protocols.
4. Record actual service time and material quantities. Estimated hours do not count as actual hours. Request approval for a new charge rather than adding an unapproved amount.
5. Complete the service, review the invoice and finalize it when the required checks are satisfied. Verified unambiguous deposits reduce the balance; exceptions go to management.
6. Confirm the displayed client or approved billing recipient, then send the invoice. Finish the after-service checklist and team hours.

## Owner

Confirm individual accounts and employee links, company contact/sender settings, billing/material rates, accounting mappings, private uploads and the shared crew guide. Review the launch checklist, financial exceptions and recovery copy. Give one person responsibility for the next action on each inquiry; use the system's recorded status rather than a separate informal list.

## Improvements to evaluate after the pilot

Measure how long it takes to prepare a quote, how often clients need help with their form, unclaimed/overdue inquiries, failed uploads and invoice/payment exceptions. Use that evidence to choose the next changes. Avoid automatic customer follow-ups until the team is comfortable with the templates and timing. Review campaigns and AI-written follow-up suggestions remain later work.
